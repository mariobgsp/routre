## FAILOVER — provider A answers 429, the client still gets 200 from B

$ curl -sD - -o /dev/null -X POST /v1/chat/completions -d @body
X-Llrouter-Cache: miss
X-Llrouter-Provider: mock-b
HTTP=200  total=0.028157s

Note: the 429 never reaches the client. The gateway tried A, saw the
rate limit, cooled A down and relayed B's answer. This is the point.

Cooldown policy is 2s base, doubling, capped at 5m
(internal/router/classify.go DefaultCooldownPolicy).
